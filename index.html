        Questions

        Is searching really free?

        Yes. You buy from the shop at its normal price. We may earn a small commission from some shops, which never changes what you pay.

        How do I pay?

        We send you a quote first. If you're happy, you pay the item price by a secure link and we order it. Our fee is a second link, sent only after it has arrived.

        What if it's late or wrong?

        You pay no fee. We'll also chase the shop or courier for you. The item itself is covered by the shop's normal returns.

        Where do you deliver?

        London, starting in Southwark and Lambeth. Send your postcode anyway and we'll tell you straight away if we can help.

        Who are you?

        A small London team. A real person handles every request right now, which is why we can reply quickly and tell you honestly when something isn't possible.
