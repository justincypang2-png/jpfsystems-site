Questions

Why not just order it myself?

You can. NoLag is for when you don't know who stocks the exact thing for same-day, and don't have twenty minutes to check six apps and three retailer sites with different cut-offs. We do that in minutes and tell you the fastest answer.

How do I pay?

Once we've found it, we send you the quote: source, arrival time, item price. You pay the item price by secure link; we place the order. The NoLag fee is a second link, sent only after it has arrived.

What if it's late or wrong?

No fee. We'll also chase the retailer or courier for you. The item itself is covered by the retailer's normal returns.

Where do you deliver?

London only for now, starting in a few boroughs. Give us your postcode and we'll tell you straight away if we can help.

Who is behind this?

A small London team testing whether people want one place that answers "who can get this to me today?". Every order is handled by a person right now, which is why we reply fast and tell you honestly when something isn't possible.
