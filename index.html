          Questions

          Is searching really free?

          Yes. You buy from the shop at its normal price. We may earn a small commission from some shops, which never changes what you pay.

          How do I pay?

          We send you a quote first. If you're happy, you pay the item price by a secure link and we order it. Our fee is a second link, sent only after it has arrived.

          What if it's late or wrong?

          You pay no fee. We'll also chase the shop or courier for you. The item itself is covered by the shop's normal returns.

          Where do you deliver?

          Anywhere in the UK. Searching works for any UK postcode. Same-day options are best in towns and cities, and there are fewer in the Scottish Highlands and islands, Northern Ireland and other islands, so we'll tell you honestly if nothing can reach you today.

          Privacy, and how NoLag makes money

          In short: no cookies, no tracking, no selling your details. We only keep what we need to run NoLag.

          Who we are

          NoLag is run by , trading as part of J.P. Freglien Systems. For anything about your details, email .

          What we collect, and why

          • Searches. What you searched for, your postcode area (like SE15, never your full postcode), which shop you tapped and when. No names, no IP addresses, no cookies. We use this to make results better and to see which shops help people.
          • Do it for me requests. The item, the delivery postcode, when you need it, your first name and your mobile or email. We use these to find and order the item for you and to keep you updated.
          • Shop listings. The shop's details and a contact name, email and phone, to check and run the listing. If a shop shares its product list, we read it every two hours in the day to show its products.
          • Use my location. Only when you tap the button. Your browser sends your location to postcodes.io, a free UK postcode service, to find the nearest postcode. NoLag never receives or keeps your location.
          • On your device only. Your language, accessibility settings, last postcode, recent searches and whether you have Amazon Prime are saved in your browser and never sent to us. Clearing this site's data removes them.

          Who helps us run NoLag

          • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Cloudflare runs the search and stores the anonymous search records. FormSubmit forwards requests and shop listings to our inbox.
          • Product photos load straight from each shop's own servers (for example Amazon or eBay), which see your browser's address like any web page does.
          • When you tap through to a shop, you're on their website and their privacy policy applies.

          How long we keep it

          • Anonymous search records: up to 24 months.
          • Do it for me requests and shop listings: up to 2 years after we last hear from you. Payment records are kept for 6 years because the law requires it.

          Your rights

          You can ask for a copy of your details, ask us to correct or delete them, or object to how we use them. Email us and we'll reply within a month. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

          Cookies

          NoLag doesn't use cookies, adverts or tracking tools, so there's no cookie banner. Our font is served from this site, not from Google.

          How NoLag makes money

          • Searching is free. When you buy through some of our links, the shop may pay us a small commission. It never changes your price. We work with Amazon Associates, eBay Partner Network, Awin and CJ.
          • As an Amazon Associate, NoLag earns from qualifying purchases.
          • Do it for me costs £2, £5 or £10, depending on the item's price, and you only pay once it has arrived on time.
          • Local shops list for free while we launch. We'll give them 30 days' notice before any fees start.

          How we choose what to show first

          • Best pick weighs price, including delivery, against how long you'd wait. Each extra day counts as about 10% of the fastest option's price (at least £3). We only pick from things that arrive within 3 days.
          • Fastest is what arrives soonest. Cheapest is the lowest total price. If a shop doesn't say what delivery costs, we compare it as if it charged a typical delivery fee, so it never looks cheaper than it is.
          • Commission never affects what we show or in what order, and no shop can pay to rank higher. If we ever show paid placements, they'll be clearly labelled "Sponsored".
